Our Story

Born from a Passion
for Beauty

Glowora was founded in 2010 in the heart of Mumbai's Zaveri Bazaar. What began as a single artisan's workshop is today a celebrated name in fine jewelry — trusted by brides, gifted at milestones, worn on life's most unforgettable days.

We believe jewelry is memory made tangible. Every ring, every chain, every stone chosen is a record of a feeling that words cannot hold.

— Priya Malhotra, Founder
